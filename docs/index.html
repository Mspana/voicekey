voicekey

Hold Space and talk to type. Hold Ctrl and talk to run a command.

A small app. One file, nothing to install. Inside dictation, the word zulu works like a key — everything before it is text, the words after it are an action.

Ask for a copy

Dictate

Hold Space — or a foot pedal — speak, let go. The words are pasted into whatever window you were already in. A short tap is still a space.

Command

Hold Ctrl and say the action: click, tab, undo, scroll up 5. Or stay in dictation and drop zulu in mid-sentence.

“ship it zulu enter” types ship it, then hits Enter.

Aim

Say zulu grid. Two numbers zoom into a cell. Repeat until you’re on the thing, then click. Two levels gets you close; three gets you to a character.

You don’t have to watch it. A high beep when it starts listening, a lower one when it captured, a long low one if something went wrong.

USB foot pedals show up as a normal key. Remap to F13 in the config next to the app and you’re done.
