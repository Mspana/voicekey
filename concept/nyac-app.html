Done

Everything the House offers, one tap away — and instant.

A ground-up redesign of the member app built around the two things members actually open it for: reserving and knowing. Try the phone — it's live.

01
Effortless for every member
Big type, plain labels, generous tap targets, and one obvious action per screen. No hunting, no fine print — dignified enough for the dining room, simple enough for a first-time visitor.
02
Distinguished, not dated
The engraved crest, an old-style serif, aged gilt hairlines, and marble grounds — the House's own materials. Pool-blue is reserved for Travers Island so campus always reads at a glance.
03
Instant, always
Screens are prefetched and cached, so navigation is immediate — never the five-second page fetch of today's app. Reservations confirm on the spot.
On the data behind it

Live club data can flow in through a sanctioned Northstar / Clubessential integration, or — permissioned with your own login — a personal sync. This prototype runs on sample data drawn from your current app so the pitch stays about the experience.

Tap “Reserve a Table,” RSVP to an event, then open Bookings.
Concept prototype. An independent redesign proposal for pitching internally, using sample data and a stylized crest — not the official NYAC app, and not affiliated with or endorsed by the Club or its software vendors. No data is collected. Palette: crimson & white with aged gold and Travers Island pool-blue.
