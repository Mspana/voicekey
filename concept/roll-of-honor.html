Done

Distinguished, effortless, and instant — the one they'll choose.

Rebuilt around reserving and knowing, in the House's own materials: engraved photography, an old-style serif, gilt hairlines, marble grounds.

Effortless
Big type, plain labels, one obvious action per screen, generous tap targets. Built for the Club's older members first.
Distinguished
Libre Caslon headlines, crimson used like ink, aged gilt rules. Pool-blue is reserved for Travers Island, so campus always reads at a glance.
Instant
Every screen is prefetched and cached — navigation is immediate, and a table confirms on the spot. Never the five-second fetch of today's app.
On the data

Live club data flows in through a sanctioned Northstar / Clubessential integration, or a permissioned personal sync. This runs on sample data so the pitch stays about the experience.

Reserve a table, RSVP to an event, then open Bookings. Try Night mode, top-right.
Concept prototype — the “just-right” direction. Sample data drawn from the current app; a hand-drawn hero stands in for the Club's own photography, which drops into the hero frame. Not the official NYAC app, and not affiliated with or endorsed by the Club or its vendors. No data is collected.
