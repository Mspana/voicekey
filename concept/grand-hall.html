The “too fancy” foil — and the likeliest runner-up. A concierge in your pocket: inscriptional capitals, Deco gold frames, a drawn façade, and a voice that says “how may we host you.” Same instant engine as the flagship.

Ceremonial
Cinzel capitals, corner-bracket frames, diamond markers. Every screen composed like an invitation — centered, measured, unhurried.
Candlelight mode
By night the cream turns to deep oxblood and the gold does the talking. Try the toggle, top-right, or House → Appearance.
